# Typical Thesis Structure in Vietnamese Universities

This document describes the standard structure of a graduation thesis (Khóa luận tốt nghiệp) commonly used in Vietnamese universities, particularly in the Faculty of Information Technology.

---

## Front Matter

### 1. Cover Page (Trang bìa)
- Ministry of Education and Training header
- University name and faculty
- Thesis title (bilingual: English + Vietnamese)
- Student name, student ID
- Supervisor name and academic title
- City and year

### 2. Inner Title Page (Trang bìa phụ)
- Same content as cover page, sometimes with additional details such as:
  - Supervisor's department/affiliation
  - Degree being pursued (e.g., Bachelor of Science in Information Technology)

### 3. Declaration of Originality (Lời cam đoan)
- A signed statement by the student confirming:
  - The work is original and conducted independently
  - All sources and references are properly cited
  - No plagiarism or fabrication of data
- Signed with student name, date, and signature

### 4. Acknowledgements (Lời cảm ơn)
- Thanks to supervisor, faculty, university
- Thanks to family, friends, classmates
- Thanks to anyone who contributed to the research

### 5. Abstract (Tóm tắt)
- **Vietnamese abstract** (Tóm tắt tiếng Việt): ~200-300 words
- **English abstract**: ~200-300 words
- Each abstract includes:
  - Research problem
  - Methodology
  - Key results
  - Conclusions
  - Keywords (4-6 keywords)

### 6. Table of Contents (Mục lục)
- All chapters, sections, and subsections with page numbers

### 7. List of Figures (Danh mục hình vẽ)
- Figure number, caption, and page number

### 8. List of Tables (Danh mục bảng biểu)
- Table number, caption, and page number

### 9. List of Abbreviations (Danh mục từ viết tắt)
- Abbreviation and full term, sorted alphabetically

## Main Body

### Chapter 1: Introduction (Mở đầu / Giới thiệu)
- **1.1 Context and Motivation** (Lý do chọn đề tài)
  - Real-world problem or need
  - Current situation and challenges
- **1.2 Research Objectives** (Mục tiêu nghiên cứu)
  - General objective
  - Specific objectives (measurable, achievable)
- **1.3 Research Questions** (Câu hỏi nghiên cứu)
  - Primary and secondary research questions
- **1.4 Scope and Limitations** (Phạm vi và giới hạn)
  - What is included and excluded
  - Constraints (time, resources, data)
- **1.5 Research Methodology Overview** (Phương pháp nghiên cứu)
  - Brief description of methods used
- **1.6 Contributions** (Đóng góp của đề tài)
  - Theoretical and practical contributions
- **1.7 Thesis Structure** (Bố cục khóa luận)
  - Brief summary of each chapter

### Chapter 2: Literature Review and Theoretical Foundations (Cơ sở lý thuyết và tổng quan tài liệu)
- **2.1 Theoretical Foundations** (Cơ sở lý thuyết)
  - Core theories, models, and concepts relevant to the research
  - Definitions of key terms
- **2.2 Related Technologies** (Các công nghệ liên quan)
  - Technologies, frameworks, and tools used or referenced
- **2.3 Related Work** (Các nghiên cứu liên quan)
  - Review of existing systems, papers, and solutions
  - Comparison and analysis of strengths/weaknesses
- **2.4 Research Gap** (Khoảng trống nghiên cứu)
  - What existing work has not addressed
  - How this thesis fills the gap

### Chapter 3: System Analysis and Design (Phân tích và thiết kế hệ thống)
- **3.1 Requirements Analysis** (Phân tích yêu cầu)
  - Functional requirements
  - Non-functional requirements
  - Use case diagrams and descriptions
- **3.2 System Architecture** (Kiến trúc hệ thống)
  - Overall architecture diagram
  - Component descriptions
  - Technology stack selection and justification
- **3.3 Database Design** (Thiết kế cơ sở dữ liệu)
  - Entity-relationship diagram (ERD)
  - Table/collection descriptions
  - Data dictionary
- **3.4 Interface Design** (Thiết kế giao diện)
  - UI/UX wireframes or mockups
  - User flow diagrams
- **3.5 Algorithm / Model Design** (Thiết kế thuật toán / mô hình)
  - Core algorithms or AI/ML models
  - Pseudocode or flowcharts
  - Mathematical formulations (if applicable)

### Chapter 4: Implementation (Triển khai / Cài đặt hệ thống)
- **4.1 Development Environment** (Môi trường phát triển)
  - Hardware and software setup
  - Development tools and versions
- **4.2 Implementation Details** (Chi tiết cài đặt)
  - Key modules and their implementation
  - Code structure and organization
  - Important code snippets with explanations
- **4.3 System Screenshots** (Giao diện hệ thống)
  - Screenshots of major features
  - Walkthrough of user workflows
- **4.4 Challenges and Solutions** (Khó khăn và giải pháp)
  - Technical challenges encountered during development
  - How they were resolved

### Chapter 5: Testing and Evaluation (Kiểm thử và đánh giá)
- **5.1 Testing Strategy** (Chiến lược kiểm thử)
  - Unit testing, integration testing, system testing
  - Test cases and test data
- **5.2 Experimental Setup** (Thiết lập thí nghiệm)
  - Environment, datasets, participants (if applicable)
  - Evaluation metrics
- **5.3 Results** (Kết quả)
  - Quantitative results with tables and charts
  - Statistical analysis (if applicable)
- **5.4 Discussion** (Thảo luận)
  - Interpretation of results
  - Comparison with existing solutions
  - Strengths and weaknesses observed

### Chapter 6: Conclusion and Future Work (Kết luận và hướng phát triển)
- **6.1 Summary of Work** (Tóm tắt công việc)
  - What was accomplished
  - How objectives were met
- **6.2 Key Findings** (Kết quả chính)
  - Most important results and insights
- **6.3 Limitations** (Hạn chế)
  - Honest assessment of limitations
- **6.4 Future Work** (Hướng phát triển)
  - Concrete suggestions for extending the research
  - Potential improvements

## Back Matter

### References (Tài liệu tham khảo)
- Formatted according to a consistent citation style
- Common styles in Vietnamese CS theses: IEEE, APA
- Sorted by citation order (IEEE) or alphabetically (APA)
- Minimum ~20-30 references for a bachelor's thesis
- Should include a mix of:
  - International journal/conference papers
  - Books and textbooks
  - Vietnamese publications (if relevant)
  - Reputable online sources

### Appendices (Phụ lục)
- **Appendix A:** Source code (key modules or full repository link)
- **Appendix B:** Survey questionnaires or interview scripts (if applicable)
- **Appendix C:** Detailed test results or raw data
- **Appendix D:** User manual or deployment guide
- **Appendix E:** Any supplementary materials

## Formatting Notes (Common Vietnamese University Standards)

| Element | Standard |
|---------|----------|
| Paper size | A4 |
| Font | Times New Roman |
| Font size | 13pt (body text) |
| Line spacing | 1.5 |
| Margins | Top: 2.5cm, Bottom: 2.5cm, Left: 3.5cm, Right: 2cm |
| Page numbers | Bottom center, starting from the first chapter |
| Chapter headings | Bold, 14-16pt, centered or left-aligned |
| Section headings | Bold, 13pt |
| Figures & Tables | Numbered by chapter (e.g., Figure 3.1, Table 4.2) |
| Total length | 50-80 pages (excluding appendices) |

## Key Differences from Western Thesis Formats

1. **Declaration of Originality** is mandatory and placed prominently in the front matter
2. **Bilingual abstract** (Vietnamese + English) is required
3. **System screenshots** are expected and valued as evidence of working implementation
4. **Implementation chapter** is typically more detailed, showing actual code and UI
5. **Practical contribution** is emphasized — a working system or prototype is expected, not just theoretical analysis
6. **Supervisor approval** signature is required on the inner title page or a separate approval sheet
7. **Defense presentation** (Bảo vệ khóa luận) — the thesis is defended before a committee of 3-5 faculty members
