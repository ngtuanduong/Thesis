Note. Two observable signals from a code submission — correctness and effort (attempts and time) — replace the self-reported recall rating used in flashcard FSRS. Time thresholds (120 s, 300 s) and the multi-attempt threshold (3) are design choices informed by typical introductory Python solve times; sensitivity is evaluated in §5.4.
